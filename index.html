AniPaint – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Thank you for using AniPaint. This policy explains how our app handles personal data.

Data We Collect

AniPaint does not collect any personal data. No account, registration, or sign-in is required. Paintings and purchase records are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Third Parties

In-app purchases are processed via Google Play Billing. For Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Our app contains no ads and uses no third-party analytics.

Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.
